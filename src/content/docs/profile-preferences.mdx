## First-use guide

Choose your role, industry, current focus, and LCA experience in separate steps. Expand **Other** if the cards do not fit. Select **Next**, go back to revise, or skip.

On the review step, decide whether to save your profile. **Fill in example** only fills the composer; it does not start a query. Accounts with conversation history or an existing profile are not prompted again.

## Edit your profile

Open the account menu at the bottom left and select **Preferences**. Existing information is filled in. **Work profile** contains your role and industry; **Answer preferences** controls length, presentation, and source requirements. Select **Save changes** when finished.

Saved information is associated with your account and can inform answers. Describe the conditions of the current question explicitly; a profile does not replace product, process, or region requirements.

## Clear your profile

Use the clear option in Preferences to remove these profile fields and answer preferences. This does not delete conversation history or other memories. Clearing a profile does not repeat completed first-use onboarding.
