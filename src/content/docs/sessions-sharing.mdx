## Start or continue

Choose **New Task** in the sidebar, or use Cmd+K on macOS and Ctrl+K on Windows. Find a conversation in the history list or search and continue asking questions. State new product, process, or region conditions in your question.

## Share a conversation

Use **Share** at the top right. Choose an expiry of 1, 3, 7, 15, or 30 days, generate the link, and copy it. Check client names, formulations, and other sensitive information before sharing.

## Revoke a link

Reopen the conversation's sharing dialog to revoke an existing link. A share link is not a permanent archive.

## Refresh access

After data access changes, use **Refresh access** in the dataset details. Returning from a credit purchase restores the original conversation. A top-up does not unlock a dataset; see [Databases and data access](/guides/database-licensing).
